## Found
- **Correction:** the 5,907 South Asian figure on the topic form counted all samples. Labelled
  South Asian isolates = **2,162** (India 1,476, Pakistan 489, Nepal 197).
- Single linkage at 12 SNPs chained lineage 2 and 4 into one 2,539-isolate component through
  mixed-lineage samples. Fixed by linking only isolates of the same single lineage.
- WHO catalogue implementation reproduces published CRyPTIC numbers (RIF 95.6% / 95.1%, INH 92.9% /
  98.4% sensitivity / specificity on all isolates).
- Aggregate transfer is fine: AUC drop on South Asian isolates ≤ 0.018.
- By lineage it is not: lineage 1 and 3 resistant isolates detected 11–41 points less than
  lineage 2 (LEV 53.6% vs 93.8%). Shared by random-split models and WHO, so it is a knowledge gap
  about these lineages, not mainly a training-origin effect.
- Regional: *embC* A387V / *embB* Q445R in a South Asian lineage 2 group, ~35 missed EMB-resistant
  isolates, absent or near-absent among resistant training isolates.
- KAN/AMI holdout: same AUC, higher sensitivity, ~10 points lower specificity — threshold shift.
- Lineage as a feature changes holdout AUC by ≤ 0.006 and leaves lineage 1 sensitivity unchanged:
  the gap is not closed by telling the model the lineage, and the models are not using lineage as
  a shortcut.

- With 95% intervals the lineage gap is statistically clear for INH, RIF, LEV and MXF; for EMB,
  ETH, KAN and AMI it points the same way but the intervals overlap (small groups).

## Decided
- Best model per drug chosen on random-split AUC (all isolates) so the holdout is never used to
  pick a model.
- Report the lineage result as the headline, with the hypothesis only partly supported.
